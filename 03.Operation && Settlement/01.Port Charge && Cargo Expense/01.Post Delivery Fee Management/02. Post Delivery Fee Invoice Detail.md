#### 3. 건수 만들기

```sql
SELECT A.DLVRY_FEE_INVO_NO
, A.ENTEPRISE_NO
, A.ACC_CODE
, ACC_NAME_FUNC( A.ACC_CODE ) AS ACC_NAME
, A.INVO_DATE
, A.REF_INVO_NO
, A.CURCY_CODE
, A.POSTING_DATE
, A.PROCESS_STS_FLAG
, User_Name_Func( A.sys_cre_user_id ) as user_name
, USERTEAM_INFO_FUNC( A.sys_cre_user_id ) as user_team_name
, User_EmpNo_Func( A.sys_cre_user_id ) as user_sabun
, COMCODE_INFO_FUNC( 'PROCESS_STS_FLAG', A.PROCESS_STS_FLAG ) AS PROCESS_STS_NAME
, A.CANCEL_FLAG
, A.EXC_DATE
, A.EXC_RATE_TYPE
, A.LOC_EXC_RATE
, A.REMARK
, A.SOURCE_SLIP_NO
, A.CANCEL_SOURCE_SLIP_NO
FROM OPD_POST_DLVRY_FEE_HEAD A
WHERE TO_CHAR( A.INVO_DATE , 'yyyyMMdd' ) >= '20200604'
AND TO_CHAR( A.INVO_DATE , 'yyyyMMdd' ) <= '20260904'
AND TO_CHAR( A.POSTING_DATE , 'yyyyMMdd' ) >= '20200604'
AND TO_CHAR( A.POSTING_DATE , 'yyyyMMdd' ) <= '20260904'
ORDER BY A.DLVRY_FEE_INVO_NO DESC
```

- 쿼리 실행 후 PROCESS_STS_FLAG 컬럼을 U 로 업데이트 처리
